Frequently Asked Questions

What qualifications are needed to enroll?

No prior barbering experience is required for introductory courses.

How long does it take to complete a program?

Basic courses last a few weeks to a few months; full certification programs take 9–12 months.

Is there financial aid available?

Scholarships, grants, and flexible payment plans are available.

What kind of job support is offered after completion?

Job placement assistance and career counseling are provided.

Can I visit the school before enrolling?

Yes, tours are available where you can meet instructors, talk with students, and see the facilities.
